More than a decade in the professional game. One fractured hand that reset everything. And, in 2026, a place in the U.S. Open field at Shinnecock.

Manav Shah earned Second-Team All-Pac-12 honors at UCLA and turned professional expecting a familiar climb. Instead, a fractured hamate hook during Korn Ferry qualifying sent him back to the beginning — and he rebuilt his swing and his game from the ground up.

What came next was a genuinely global career — a Quito Open title in Latin America, full status on the Asian Tour and India's IGPL, and a game sharp enough to reach the sport's biggest stage: the 126th U.S. Open at Shinnecock Hills.

The son of Bakersfield hospitality entrepreneurs, Manav brings an owner's mindset to the course: a powerful, accurate swing, world-class hands around the green, and the composure to compete anywhere in the world.
